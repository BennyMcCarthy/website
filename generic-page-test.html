This year, have taken note whenever I thought of something where I believe there could be room for improvement, and I'd appreciate if you would consider some of my feedback.

Preamble

Should any words read as harmful or cold, please be assured that is not my intention whatsoever. No part of this document is intended to be read as personal, pretentious, or overreaching, and all comments are made in good faith, I just really do care about this. I greatly enjoy my time in class and appreciate all the time and genuine effort teachers give to students, but believe that there is room for meaningful improvement, and I would hate to see things continue as they are when I believe there is opportunity for things to be so much better.

I would also like to note that I do not consider myself anywhere near 'good', and genuinely think my skills sit at what I believe should be an average level for someone my age. There is no question that an interest and passion in the subject plays a great part in eagerness to improve, however if lessons are made unsubstantial and students do not feel as if they are growing, then this excitement will be not be there. I genuinely believe *all* students should be able to reach 'my level', and not be the exception but a baseline. Art knowledge does not come from the heavens to a lucky few, but can and should be taught. I see the word 'talent' as an insult; it suggests the artist does not make decisive and considered actions, but has a mindless privilege, and they create exceptional results without putting effort or any thought behind their work. It also implies that you cannot learn to make a good picture. Through this document, I hope to prove how this is false.

I obviously have no teaching experience, and am unaware of practices in this field that may have influenced some of the things I take issue to, but I would like to believe that I would be able to give some value from my own experience from what I have picked up in class at school, and at home online. I am extremely fortunate to live in the age of the internet, where direct words from the best artists alive right now are accessible for free online, and not heard second-hand from a school.

I am also not fully aware about what decisions about the course are able to be made by the Long Bay College faculty, and what is governed by NZQA. I am able to make some assumptions of how stuff goes, but obviously no clear picture. *Should there be feedback in this document that the LBC faculty agrees on but does not have control over, I would be more than happy to give any assistance I can to getting these changes considered by NZQA, should you require anything such as written student voice. These things need to change.*

Lastly, I would also like to note that in year 9-10 I took 'Digital Art' instead of 'Art', so some of the things I propose that should be taught may already be in effect, and I just never got to see that. Similarly, the classes across all years from 9-12 may have undergone changes since I sat them that I am unaware of. Everything here is just from the recollection of my personal experience. I also have never taken Photography in school as a subject, so I won't be talking about it.
