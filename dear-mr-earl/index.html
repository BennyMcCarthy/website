Dear Mr Earl is a 2D animated feature that follows Postman Harry Bodeman's unsuspected adventure as he sets off to find his childhood friend Mr Earl, who was cut from the postal service just before the publishing of his fantastic new storybook.

This is my year 13 NCEA New Zealand's high school curriculum / qualification (National Certificate of Educational Achievement) Design project that's being made right now, and is due to release this November as a 'moving image'.
