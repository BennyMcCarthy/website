    This is a collection of 3D minecraft items I made for various resourcepacks. Some are for my own packs, but most are a made for Cyber's Minecraft refreshed, a pack that adds 3d models to every item in the game, but all the stuff you see on this page is my own. It has been a lot of fun doing items because they all are all quite small so you don't get bored making them and can just move onto the next one quite quickly. I won't try and pretend that Actions & Stuff wasn't a massive inspiration for the 3D items, but I try to come up with my own different way of interpreting the item sprites instead of just copying what someone else has come up with without trying something else first, even if the result may be pretty similar since they are from the same reference.
