3D Items

Return

This is a collection of 3D minecraft items I made for various resourcepacks. Some are for my own packs, but most are a made with Team Disco, but everything you see here is my own work.

It has been a lot of fun doing items because they all are all quite small so you don't get bored making them and can just move onto the next one quite quickly. I will also acknowledge the obvious inspiration from the awesome pack Actions & Stuff, but I always try to come up with my own different way of interpreting the item sprites instead of just copying what someone else has done, even if the result may be pretty similar since they are from the same reference.


These are some of my favourite items I did, but you can see them all below.
