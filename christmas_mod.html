christmas mod

During christas time, none of my friends and I could agree on what Chritsmas mod to get on our Minecraft server, so I thought it would be fun to make my own.

I am happy with how it turned out as my first mod, and developing it was straightforward enough. Having mods follow the same asset structure as resourcepacks made things easier.

Unfortunately, I was being a slacker and finished most of the features just before and even after Christmas. I hope to continue development of the mod during Christmas time in 2026, and port it to the latest Minectaft version. Below is a list of everything in the mod.
