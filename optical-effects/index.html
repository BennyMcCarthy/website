My eyes have problems that can cause cool looking effects, so I decided to make them the theme for my NCEA New Zealand's high school curriculum / qualification (National Certificate of Educational Achievement) L2 Painting board.

This includes astigmatism, halos, visual snow, exposure, and bokeh that you get from squinting eyelashes. Really observing my vision was quite cool, but the actual paintings turned out pretty crap. They all were very rushed and I hardly put any thought into composition or anything and I think it definitely shows.
