dear-mr-earl

Dear Mr Earl is a 2D animated feature that follows Postman Harry Bodeman's unsuspected adventure as he sets off to find his childhood friend Mr Earl, and get his fantastic new storybook published after he was cut off from the postal service.

This is my year 13 NCEA New Zealand's high school curriculum / qualification (National Certificate of Educational Achievement) Design project that's being made right now, and is due to release this November.
