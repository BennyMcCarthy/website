Here you can find a collection of links to (free) learning resources related to painting I have found to be genuinely useful and insightful.

If you have any suggestions to add to the list, please contact me





Noob

I've structured this list so it starts with first being able to make any image, and then at the end, what makes a great image. This section will focus on being able to capture the light and colour of a scene, then how to decisively design the composition and stuff.

I know its called a study, but they are really fun and the best and only way you can get a good grasp of this stuff. Try a few and you will be surprised at how much improvement you can make in such little time.




Pro

These videos are really great at showing you exactly how you should be thinking when approaching a painting. Nothing is done 'just because', and every element is carefully designed.





Hacker

This is now going into more specific design features you can be considering adding.

These videos from Alfredo Cassano are really great at showing what good drawing looks like, and how you should approach it. I've timestamped some specific moments of interest, but the whole video is worth watching.
